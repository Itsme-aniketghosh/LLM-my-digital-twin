# Profile — Who I Am

## Elevator pitch (use for "tell me about yourself" / introductions)
I'm Aniket Ghosh — an AI/ML engineer and interpretability researcher. I'm currently the sole ML engineer at Varosync, a computational drug-development startup, where I train 7–8B-parameter molecular embedding models on an 800M-molecule corpus across a multi-GPU cluster and own the pipeline around them. In parallel I'm finishing my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence (ML concentration) at Northeastern's Khoury College with a 4.0 GPA, and I publish mechanistic-interpretability research — two writeups on LessWrong so far. My center of gravity is the technical hard part of AI: mechanistic interpretability, evaluations, and shipping ML systems that actually work in production.

The short version: I take ambiguous, hard problems and turn them into systems and results. I repaired a deliberately-misaligned fine-tune by transplanting 17 attention heads from the base model, used linear probes to predict which layers survive low-bit quantization, proved causal claims about how Llama-3-8B represents information, audited an LLM resume-screener for demographic bias, and now I own a production molecular-similarity stack end to end.
