## Published Work

### 1. Intelligent Traffic Sign Detection System
**Status:** Published at AISC 2024 (Artificial Intelligence and Soft Computing Conference)
**Publisher:** Springer's "Information and Communication Technologies" series
**Co-authors:** Research team at IEM Kolkata

#### Problem Statement:
Traffic sign detection systems trained on Western datasets (like Berkeley Driving Dataset) perform poorly on Indian roads due to:
- Cluttered backgrounds
- Different sign designs
- Varied lighting conditions
- Occlusion from vehicles and pedestrians
- Diverse road conditions

#### Technical Approach:
1. **Base Detection:** Used YOLOv8 for initial object detection
2. **Custom CNN Filter:** Developed additional CNN layer to filter false positives
3. **Two-stage Training:**
   - First trained CNN on Berkeley Driving Dataset
   - Re-trained on IIIT Hyderabad Indian Driving Dataset
4. **Joint Detection-Filtering Pipeline:** Combined YOLO detection with CNN filtering

#### Key Innovations:
- Addressed region-specific challenges in traffic sign detection
- Created hybrid architecture combining YOLO and CNN
- Achieved significant accuracy improvement for Indian road conditions
- Reduced false positives in cluttered environments

#### Datasets Used:
- Berkeley Driving Dataset (BDD100K)
- IIIT Hyderabad Indian Driving Dataset

#### Results:
- Improved detection accuracy for Indian road conditions
- Robust performance in cluttered environments
- Demonstrated importance of region-specific model adaptation

## In Progress Research

### 2. Cell Segmentation Pipeline for Histopathological Images
**Status:** Research completed, paper preparation in progress
**Institution:** Jadavpur University
**Supervisors:** Dr. Kaushiki Roy, Dr. Debotosh Bhattacharjee

#### Problem Statement:
Automated cell segmentation in histopathological images is challenging due to:
- Densely packed cell colonies
- Overlapping cell boundaries
- Variable cell sizes and shapes
- Staining variations
- Complex tissue backgrounds

#### Technical Approach:
1. **Preprocessing Pipeline:**
   - Color quantization to normalize staining variations
   - Contrast enhancement for cell boundary visibility
   
2. **Clustering for Cell Detection:**
   - DBSCAN (Density-Based Spatial Clustering) for identifying cell clusters
   - Handles arbitrary-shaped cell groups
   - Robust to noise and outliers

3. **Segmentation:**
   - Combined classical CV techniques with deep learning
   - Custom pipeline for densely packed cells
   - Morphological operations for boundary refinement

#### Applications:
- Disease diagnosis assistance
- Cell counting automation
- Treatment response monitoring (rising/falling cell counts)
- Pathology workflow enhancement

#### Technical Details:
- Implemented in Python with OpenCV and PyTorch
- Tested on multiple cell line datasets
- Focused on confluent cellular colonies
